Frequently asked questions

How many sessions do I need?

We recommend a course of 5–10 sessions, once or twice a week. Results vary from person to person.

Does it hurt?

It's firm, but we adjust the pressure at any time. Slight redness afterwards is normal.

When shouldn't I have this massage?

For example during pregnancy, with varicose veins, skin injuries or after surgery. If in doubt, please ask your doctor first.

Is it covered by health insurance?

No. It's a cosmetic wellness treatment, not a medical massage.

What should I bring?

Nothing. Disposable underwear and towels are provided.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, using the link in your confirmation. Please let us know as early as you can, so someone else can take the slot.
